Institutional Attendance Regulation & Policy Summary
≥ 75% Exam Clearance

Students with 75% or higher cumulative class and laboratory attendance are unconditionally cleared to appear in Semester Final Examinations.

65% - 74% Medical Condonation

Students falling between 65% and 74% due to medical conditions must submit certified hospital documents to the Dean of Academic Affairs within 7 days.

< 65% Debarment Clause

Attendance below 65% results in automatic semester course debarment (Grade 'FA' - Failure due to Attendance). The student must re-register the course.
